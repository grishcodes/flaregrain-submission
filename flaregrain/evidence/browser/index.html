Reported measurement

BASS-II oxygen margin

A ¼-inch PMMA rod showed steady flame spread at 13.6% oxygen in microgravity. NASA reports a 16% oxygen blowoff limit in normal gravity for the same rod.

The comparison applies to this material, geometry, and flow. The slide gives no minimum oxygen level for microgravity flame spread.

Missing numeric value

Extinction after airflow shutoff

The BASS-II B1 and Saffire I-1 reports describe prompt extinction after shutoff. Both descriptions are qualitative. Saffire’s 420 seconds is the run duration up to shutoff.
