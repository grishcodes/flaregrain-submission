# Notice

## NASA data

Flaregrain's data comes from NASA's Physical Sciences Informatics (PSI) repository. Its combustion science area holds 24 investigations:

- **23 of them** carry the licence **CC0-1.0** on their PSI record.
- **PSI-10** (ACME/Flame Design) has a **blank licence field** on its PSI record.

The records and comparisons in `data/compare-data.json` don't use PSI-10. The intake scripts in `intake/` download all 24 investigations from NASA into a local folder that is not committed. That includes PSI-10.

NASA's experimental tables and report PDFs are not copied into this repository. Each value links to the NASA table or report page it came from. The 9 NASA reports the app cites are listed in `intake/build_corpus.py`, with their NASA Technical Reports Server or nasa.gov addresses. Answers and records cite them by page.

NASA does not endorse Flaregrain.

## AI use

- **Code:** most of the code was written with AI coding assistants, Claude Code (Anthropic) and Codex (OpenAI), working under the team's direction and review. Commits written this way carry a `Co-Authored-By` trailer. [PROVENANCE.md](PROVENANCE.md) gives the dates and authors of each part.
- **Saved explanations:** the saved comparison explanations were written by the open model qwen3:4b, running locally through Ollama 0.40.16. Each sentence was kept only if the sentence checker matched it to the record fields it cites. The app always labels these sentences "Flaregrain reading · not NASA evidence".
- **NASA values:** the hand-checked NASA values were checked by the team against the original report pages. The values come from the reports and the PSI archive. They were not generated by a model.
